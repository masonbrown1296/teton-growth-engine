# Skill: Conference Landing Page Copy

## Purpose
Generate copy for a conference-specific landing page. Can produce a 
default version (for all conference attendees) or an operator-specific 
variant (for targeted ads).

## Inputs to Read
1. brand/positioning.md
2. brand/proof-points.md
3. conference/nic-fall-2026/context.md
4. (Optional) operator profile.md for operator-specific variant

## Output Format

### Default Version
- Headline (under 10 words)
- Subhead (under 25 words)
- Section 1: The problem (3-4 sentences on what operators are missing)
- Section 2: How Teton works (3-4 sentences, focus on outcomes not features)
- Section 3: Proof (2 proof points with specific numbers)
- CTA: "Book 15 Minutes With Our Team at NIC" + booking link placeholder
- Trust bar: Customer logos placeholder

### Operator-Specific Variant
Same structure but:
- Headline references their specific pain point
- Proof points selected for their acuity mix
- If competitive displacement: includes a "Beyond [Current Solution]" section
- Language matches what we know about their priorities from research

## Output Location
Save to output/ folder as landing-page-copy.md (default) or 
operator's output/ folder (variant)
