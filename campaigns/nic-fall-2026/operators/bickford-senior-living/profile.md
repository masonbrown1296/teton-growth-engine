# Bickford Senior Living: Verified Research

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 1991 by Don and Judie Eby, for their mother Mary Bickford (diagnosed with Alzheimer's)
- **HQ:** Olathe, Kansas (13795 S Mur-Len Rd, Ste 301)
- **Communities:** 52-60+ (varies by source and date; A Place for Mom lists 52, NIC 2026 bio says "more than 60," SeniorLiving.org says 61)
- **States:** Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Nebraska, Ohio, and more (some sources say 8, others 10, including GA, PA, TN, VA)
- **Employees:** ~509 corporate (per RocketReach), ~1,037 total (per RocketReach management page)
- **Revenue:** ~$64.9M (2026, per RocketReach)
- **Ownership:** 100% family-owned and operated. Private pay only.
- **Acuity types:** Independent living, assisted living, Montessori memory care
- **Operating system:** Uses Entrepreneurial Operating System (EOS)
- **Leadership development:** Stagen Leadership Academy partner
- **Values alignment:** Active members of Conscious Capitalism movement
- **Sources:** Bickford About Us page, A Place for Mom, SeniorLiving.org, NIC 2026 speaker bio, Crunchbase, CBInsights

## The Eby Family and SLTC Structure

Bickford is one piece of a larger ecosystem Andy Eby is building:

- **Bickford Senior Living:** The operating company (communities, staff, residents)
- **Senior Living Transformation Company (SLTC):** Investment and transformation vehicle Andy co-founded. Invests in senior living properties and companies to "create value and alignment across ownership, operations, and health care." Has a $300M-$600M pipeline for expansion (SHN, May 2026). Partnered with Omega Healthcare Investors.
- **Serviam Care Network:** Andy is co-founder. Brings senior living operators together into a collaborative network for value-based care.
- **Centered Care:** The tech-enabled care platform within SLTC. CEO is Jim Lydiard. Operators gain access to an "integrated healthcare platform that uses data" to manage resident care. Started with a 113-bed community in Brentwood, TN (2023).

Sources: Senior Housing News (May 2026), McKnight's (June 2025), NIC 2026 bio, Crunchbase

## Competitive Dynamics for Teton

**Current SafelyYou customer:**
- SafelyYou provides reactive fall detection via wall-mounted cameras
- Uses video review after falls occur
- Good product, but fundamentally reactive: detects falls after they happen
- Teton's positioning: proactive ambient intelligence that predicts and prevents, not just detects

**August Health customer and investor:**
- Teton has a bidirectional EHR integration with August Health (Feb 2026)
- This is the bridge: Teton data flows into the system Bickford already uses
- No switching cost on the EHR side, just an additional data source

**Andy Eby's own words work against SafelyYou and for Teton:**
- He calls senior care "reactive, expensive, and siloed"
- He wants "proactive healthcare"
- He wants to "think differently" with AI and data
- He invested in August Health specifically because their AI is "elegant and grounded"
- Teton's entire positioning is proactive care. Andy's own philosophy aligns with Teton more than with SafelyYou's reactive model.

**The displacement pitch:**
SafelyYou tells you when someone fell. Teton tells you they're going to fall, monitors sleep disruption, tracks mobility decline, measures respiration changes, and documents staff response times. It's the difference between a smoke detector and a full building management system. And it plugs directly into the August Health EHR Bickford already runs.
