## Key Messaging Angle

Andy Eby has spent three years telling the senior living industry that care is "reactive, expensive, and siloed" and that operators need to "think differently" with AI and data. He invested his own money in August Health because their approach to embedded intelligence is, in his words, "elegant and grounded, not built on hype." He adopted SafelyYou because 92% of falls in his communities were going unwitnessed.

SafelyYou solved the detection problem. It tells Bickford when someone fell. Teton solves the prediction problem. It tells Bickford someone is going to fall, tracks the sleep disruption and mobility decline that precede it, measures respiration changes, and documents staff response times. SafelyYou is the smoke detector. Teton is the full building management system.

The messaging should never ask Andy to abandon SafelyYou. Instead, it should acknowledge that SafelyYou was the right move when Bickford needed fall detection, then position Teton as the proactive ambient intelligence layer that matches the philosophy Andy has been publicly championing. The August Health integration eliminates the usual barrier to adding new clinical technology: Teton data flows bidirectionally into the EHR Bickford already operates. No new system to learn, no new dashboard, no switching cost on the data side.

For the SLTC expansion pipeline, the angle shifts from retrofit to greenfield standard. Every new community SLTC develops or acquires needs ambient intelligence from opening day. Positioning Teton as the default technology partner for new builds converts a one-time sale into a growth-linked relationship that scales with Bickford's own expansion.
