## How It Works

- **One sensor per room.** A small sensor and edge compute unit installs in each resident room. No wearables, nothing for residents to put on or charge, nothing to forget.
- **AI runs on device.** 3D reconstruction, pose estimation, and action recognition all happen on the compute unit in the room. Raw images are analyzed, meaningful data is extracted, and the raw image is deleted. No video ever leaves the room and nothing touches the cloud. When families ask about cameras, the answer is simple: there is no video to see.
- **Continuous monitoring, not just fall detection.** Teton tracks mobility trends, sleep quality, respiration patterns, and staff interactions 24 hours a day using infrared for overnight coverage. Alerts go to care teams when patterns change, not after an incident has already occurred.
- **Fleet management built for scale.** Smith, Teton's open-source fleet management tool (built in Rust), gives IT full transparency into device health across your entire portfolio. Remote management, 99%+ uptime, no truck rolls.

## Going Beyond SafelyYou

SafelyYou is good at what it does. Teton does not replace fall detection. It adds the layer SafelyYou was never designed to provide. In addition to fall detection, Teton delivers continuous sleep monitoring, respiration tracking, mobility trend analysis, and predictive alerts that flag risk before an incident happens. SafelyYou reviews video after a fall. Teton shows your care team that a resident's gait changed three days ago, that their nighttime restlessness increased this week, or that staff check-ins on the overnight shift dropped below threshold.

Teton also addresses the privacy concern your families are already raising. SafelyYou captures and transmits video to the cloud for review. Teton processes everything on-edge, in the room, and only structured data leaves the device. No video, no cloud storage, no exposure.

This is not a rip-and-replace. SafelyYou can stay. Teton adds the proactive intelligence layer that turns your communities from reactive monitoring into the kind of data-driven, preventive care model you are already building through Centered Care and Serviam.
