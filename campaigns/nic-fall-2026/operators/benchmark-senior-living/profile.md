# Benchmark Senior Living: Verified Research

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 1997 by Tom Grape
- **HQ:** Waltham, MA (201 Jones Rd, Ste 300)
- **Communities:** ~66-70 across 9 states (CT, ME, MA, NH, NY, NJ, RI, VA, VT)
- **Associates:** ~6,300
- **AUM:** $2.5B+
- **Ownership:** Privately held
- **Acuity types:** Independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing, CCRCs
- **Sources:** Company website, Seniorly, SeniorLiving.org, Argentum

## Recent Activity

- Surpassed 90% census in 2024
- Expanding beyond the Northeast for the first time with a D.C. development (announced SHN BUILD, Nov 2025)
- New NJ community under construction (announced June 2025): five-and-a-half-acre campus offering independent living, assisted living, and memory care
- Joint venture with National Development for acquisitions (Church Hill Village, Newtown CT acquired 2025)
- 15 consecutive years named Boston Globe Top Places to Work
- Sources: SHN BUILD conference, Benchmark press releases, Multi-Housing News
