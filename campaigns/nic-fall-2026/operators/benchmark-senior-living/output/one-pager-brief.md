## How It Works

- **One sensor per room.** A small device and edge compute unit install in each resident room. No wearables for residents to remove, lose, or forget to charge.
- **AI runs on device.** 3D reconstruction, pose estimation, and action recognition all happen on the in-room compute unit. Raw images are analyzed, structured data is extracted, and the raw image is deleted. No video ever leaves the room.
- **Continuous ambient intelligence.** Teton interprets mobility, sleep quality, respiration, and staff interactions 24 hours a day. Care teams receive alerts when patterns shift, not just when something goes wrong.
- **Fleet management built for IT.** Smith, Teton's open-source fleet management tool (built in Rust), gives your IT team full transparency into device health, updates, and uptime across every community in your portfolio.

## Going Beyond SafelyYou

In addition to fall detection, Teton provides continuous ambient intelligence that covers the full spectrum of resident health signals. Where SafelyYou alerts your team after a fall has occurred and reviews video after the fact, Teton identifies the mobility pattern change three days before the fall happens. Teton's privacy architecture works differently: all AI processing runs on device in the resident's room, and no video is ever transmitted to the cloud. For a portfolio expanding into new markets, Teton offers a single platform that replaces point solutions with proactive, predictive care intelligence across falls, sleep, respiration, and staff interactions.
