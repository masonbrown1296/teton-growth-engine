## Rationale

Benchmark Senior Living qualifies as Tier 1 on three independent criteria. First, their portfolio of 66-70 communities across nine states far exceeds the 20-community threshold, and active expansion into D.C. and New Jersey adds greenfield installation opportunities. ## Key Messaging Angle

Benchmark's VP of IT said publicly that the industry can now "focus on care instead of administrative work by leveraging AI and predictive models." Their CEO has said that failing to adopt technology means "becoming a dinosaur." Yet their current fall-detection vendor, SafelyYou, is architecturally reactive: it records video, sends it to the cloud, and reviews it after a fall has already happened.

The core message to Benchmark is that Teton closes the gap between their stated ambition and their current technology stack. Specifically:

- **Predictive vs. reactive.** SafelyYou detects falls after they occur. Teton identifies deteriorating mobility, irregular sleep patterns, and changes in activity levels before a fall happens. This is the "predictive model" Elalamy asked for.
- **Privacy architecture.** SafelyYou sends video to the cloud. Teton processes everything on-edge with no video leaving the room. For an operator expanding into new states with varying privacy regulations, this matters.
- **Ambient breadth.** SafelyYou started in memory care fall detection and is expanding outward. Teton is a full ambient intelligence platform from the start, covering falls, sleep, mobility, respiration, and staff interactions across all acuity levels. Benchmark operates IL, AL, MC, SNF, and CCRCs, so they need a platform that works across their entire portfolio, not just memory care.
- **VBC infrastructure.** Grape has called healthcare-senior living integration "inevitable." Continuous ambient data is the infrastructure layer that makes value-based care reporting possible. Teton provides that layer. SafelyYou does not.
- **Greenfield expansion.** The new D.C. and NJ communities are opportunities to install Teton from day one, avoiding the retrofit complexity of existing buildings.
