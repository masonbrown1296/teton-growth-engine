## Recommendations

### 1. Reallocate Meta budget toward proactive-vs-reactive and Sagora proof point creatives

The top two creatives (proof-sagora-74pct at 3.88% CTR and proactive-vs-reactive at 3.87% CTR) are performing at roughly 2x the rate of the weakest creative (proof-aquinas-adoption at 1.50%). Shift 30-40% of spend away from the Aquinas adoption creative and the Physical AI category creative toward the two top performers. Current combined Meta spend across both operators is $1,979.70. The Aquinas adoption creative and category-physical-ai creative account for roughly $700 of that with below-average returns.

**Specific reallocation:** Pause proof-aquinas-adoption as a standalone Meta ad. Repurpose the Aquinas adoption story exclusively for email sequences and LinkedIn posts, where narrative-form content performs better for that proof point. Increase spend on proactive-vs-reactive for Benchmark audiences (this creative has only run for Bickford so far but directly addresses the SafelyYou switching conversation). Test the August Health integration creative for Benchmark, since they are also on August Health.

### 2. Fix the NIC meeting ask in email sequences

Touch 3 ("15 minutes at NIC?") had the worst performance across both operators. John Hartmayer did not open it. Andy Eby opened but did not click. Joe Eby did not open it. The only success was Moulay Elalamy. The direct meeting ask in a subject line is getting filtered or ignored, especially by C-suite contacts who receive dozens of similar NIC meeting requests.

**Specific fix:** Replace the Touch 3 subject line with something that provides value, not an ask. Example: "The data from our first 6 months at Sagora (before/after)" or "What 83% fall reduction looks like across 6 countries." Embed the NIC meeting CTA inside the email body after delivering the value, not in the subject line.

### 3. Create content that addresses the "SafelyYou coexistence" question

Both Benchmark and Bickford are SafelyYou customers. Both are interested in Teton but have switching cost concerns. There is no content asset that directly addresses this. Andy Eby described SafelyYou as "good at what it does" but wants proactive capability that SafelyYou does not offer. John Hartmayer visited the case study and pricing pages, which suggests he is evaluating whether the investment makes sense alongside an existing solution.

**Specific action:** Create a one-page comparison or positioning piece: "Already Using Fall Detection? Here's What Ambient Intelligence Adds." This is not a competitive teardown. It frames Teton as a layer on top of reactive solutions, addressing sleep monitoring, respiration tracking, and proactive risk identification that camera-based fall detection does not cover. Use the Sagora proof point (74% fall reduction) alongside a clear statement that Teton does not require replacing existing fall detection. This asset fills the most obvious content gap across both active pipelines.

### 4. Assign a rep to Benchmark immediately and update the HubSpot record

Benchmark is a Tier 1 target with 66-70 communities, an active VP of IT who booked an NIC meeting, a COO who clicked through to a case study and visited the pricing page, and an unidentified visitor browsing from Benchmark's IP range. Despite all this, the HubSpot record shows "unassigned" rep and "new" deal stage. This should be moved to at least "engaged" stage and assigned before NIC. Moulay Elalamy's meeting booking should be logged as a conversion event.

### 5. Re-engage Bickford through Simone Pena, not just the Eby family

Andy Eby is the champion but has gone dark on sales outreach. Joe Eby appears to be a passive blocker with implementation concerns. Simone Pena (VP of Sales and Marketing) has shown independent engagement: 3 Meta ad clicks and a 4:05 website session on September 30 reading both the Sagora and Aquinas case studies. She has not been included in the email sequence.

**Specific action:** Add Simone Pena to a tailored email sequence that leads with marketing-relevant proof points: family satisfaction, census impact, competitive differentiation for Bickford's sales team. If Simone becomes an internal advocate alongside Andy, the deal has two champions instead of one. This also routes around Joe Eby's implementation fatigue objection by building the case from a revenue/marketing angle rather than an operations angle.

### 6. Prepare NIC-specific materials for both operators

NIC Fall 2026 is the most important near-term conversion event for both pipelines. Moulay Elalamy (Benchmark) has booked a meeting. Andy Eby (Bickford) has not, but is attending and still engaging with content.

**For the Benchmark meeting with Moulay:** Prepare a technical walkthrough focused on privacy architecture (he visited that page), August Health integration (relevant to their stack), and on-edge compute (aligns with his IT role). Bring the Sagora data since that proof point drove his highest ad engagement. Have a business case ready for John Hartmayer if he joins.
