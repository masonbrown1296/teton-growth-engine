# Teton: Brand Positioning Layer

## What Teton Is
Teton is the proactive care platform for senior living. The system installs 
a small sensor and compute unit in resident rooms that continuously interprets 
activity and health signals: mobility, sleep, respiration, staff interactions. 
Insights are delivered to care teams through the Teton application.

## What Teton Is NOT
- Not a fall detection vendor (competitors like SafelyYou are reactive, 
  detecting falls after they happen)
- Not a camera system (no video ever leaves the room, privacy-preserving 
  by design)
- Not a wearable (nothing for residents to put on, charge, or forget)

## Core Positioning
"Other tools alert you when something goes wrong. Teton shows you the 
pattern before it becomes a problem."

Teton gives care teams continuous visibility into what is happening across 
their communities. This is the shift from reactive monitoring to proactive 
ambient intelligence.

## Privacy Architecture (Key Differentiator)
- Sensor captures visual data including infrared (24hr coverage)
- Hardwired to on-edge compute unit in the room (never touches network)
- AI models run ON DEVICE: 3D reconstruction, pose estimation, action recognition
- Raw image is analyzed, meaningful data extracted, raw image deleted
- Only structured events and metrics leave the room
- Incident review shows anonymized animated reconstruction, not real video
- Open-source fleet management tool (Smith, built in Rust) for IT transparency

## Category Creation
The concept is "physical AI": AI that operates in and understands physical 
spaces. Most buyers think AI means ChatGPT. The education challenge is 
translating sensors to application layer to care team notifications. 
Marketing must educate the category, not just generate demand.
