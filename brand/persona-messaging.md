# Teton: Persona-Specific Messaging

## COO / VP of Operations
**What they care about:** Operational efficiency, liability reduction, 
staffing ROI, consistency across shifts and communities, portfolio-wide visibility
**Lead with:** "One dashboard showing what's happening across every community, 
every shift. No more relying on shift change notes or incident reports filed 
hours after the fact."
**Proof points:** 28% staff retention, 5x ROI, portfolio-wide visibility
**Objection to address:** "We already have SafelyYou / a fall detection system"
**Response:** "SafelyYou tells you when someone fell. Teton tells you the mobility 
pattern changed three days ago. It's the difference between a smoke detector 
and a building management system."

## VP of IT / Director of Technology
**What they care about:** Architecture, integration, privacy/security, 
implementation burden, interoperability with existing systems
**Lead with:** "On-edge compute. No video leaves the room. AI runs on device. 
Open-source fleet management. Integrates with August Health and other EHRs."
**Proof points:** Privacy architecture, Smith (open-source, Rust), Nvidia partnership
**Objection to address:** "How does this integrate with our existing stack?"
**Response:** "Bidirectional EHR integration with August Health already live. 
The sensor is a single install per room. Fleet managed remotely via Smith 
with 99%+ uptime."

## Director of Nursing / VP of Clinical / CNO
**What they care about:** Staff burden, training time, clinical documentation, 
whether it actually helps frontline workers or adds more to their plate
**Lead with:** "Your night nurse covering 40 rooms can't be everywhere. 
Teton watches what she can't and alerts her when something needs attention. 
No training beyond reading a notification."
**Proof points:** 95% response time improvement (Sagora), 99.8% adoption (Aquinas)
**Objection to address:** "My staff won't use another system"
**Response:** "Aquinas hit 99.8% adoption across their Pennsylvania communities. 
It's not another system to learn. It's a notification that says 'Room 214, 
check on Mrs. Rodriguez, mobility pattern changed overnight.'"

## CFO / Owner / Asset Manager
**What they care about:** ROI, insurance impact, occupancy, liability, CapEx
**Lead with:** "Falls cost senior living communities $380,000 per year on average. 
Teton customers see 74-83% fewer falls and 5x ROI within the first year."
**Proof points:** 5x ROI, fall reduction stats, staff retention (turnover is expensive)
**Objection to address:** "What does this cost per room?"
**Response:** Position against the cost of NOT having it: one fall-related 
lawsuit, one wrongful death claim, one family pulling a resident.
