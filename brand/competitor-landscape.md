# Teton: Competitive Landscape

## SafelyYou
- **What they do:** AI-powered fall detection using wall-mounted cameras. 
  Video review after falls. Remote clinical support. Recently added Clarity 
  (automated care tracking) and Aware (predictive insights).
- **Funding:** $133M+ total, Series C ($43M, Jan 2025) led by Touring Capital
- **Scale:** 800+ communities, 5,000+ residents
- **Positioning:** "Reduce falls, risk, and cost in senior living"
- **Key customers:** Benchmark, Bickford, StoryPoint, Cogir, Sonida, ProMedica, 
  Maplewood, ONELIFE, Milestone, MorningStar
- **Teton advantage:** SafelyYou is fundamentally reactive (detects falls after 
  they happen, reviews video after the fact). Teton is proactive (continuous 
  ambient intelligence, predictive alerts). SafelyYou uses video that goes to 
  cloud. Teton processes on-edge, no video leaves the room. SafelyYou started 
  in memory care fall detection and is expanding outward. Teton started as a 
  full ambient intelligence platform.
- **SafelyYou advantage over Teton:** Deeper U.S. penetration, larger install 
  base, more established brand, trusted by 50% of largest providers. Longer 
  track record of published outcomes.

## Inspiren
- **What they do:** Ambient monitoring for senior living
- **Notable:** Fast Company Most Innovative Companies 2026
- **Teton advantage:** Less publicly available outcome data

## CarePredict
- **What they do:** Wearable-based (Tempo wrist device) monitoring. Fall detection, 
  wander management, real-time location, predictive health monitoring.
- **Teton advantage:** CarePredict requires a wearable. Residents with dementia 
  remove wearables. Teton is ambient, nothing to wear.

## Sage (new entrant)
- **What they do:** New competitor, two founders with clinical backgrounds from NYC
- **Per Chris (Teton):** "Their stated objective is totally different. They want 
  to build and sell as quickly as possible." Formidable but different philosophy.
